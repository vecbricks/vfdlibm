# Why:         cosh has two arms that differ only on |x| < 0.5*ln2 (e_cosh.c line 62): slice
#              computes that range in the vector, from s_expm1.c's k == 0 lines; finish finishes
#              its lanes per lane by StrictMath.cosh. How often a vector holds such a lane is the
#              share of the range among the inputs: 3.5% in Varka's baseline benchmark, 17% in
#              Spark's unit tests (issue #32), so CoshKindsBench has those two shares, at exactly
#              36 and 174 places of 1024; CoshBenchmark's common data has 0.6%.
# Commit:      eeaf3ff135b7070e2d1ca189f280076946005d24
# Tracked:     1 tracked file differed from it when the run started: SKILLS.md, a
#              document (its budget text). At that commit Cosh.cosh named the finish arm; the rows
#              call coshSlice and coshFinish directly, so the bodies measured are the final
#              ones, and the change since is which arm Cosh.cosh names.

# CPU:         AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX PRO 370 w/ Radeon 890M
